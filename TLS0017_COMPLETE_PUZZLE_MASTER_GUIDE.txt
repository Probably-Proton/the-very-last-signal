the historical Case 17 records repository: historical documents were not being destroyed, but their

Mercer was found dead near the Presidio Heights district of San Francisco. Dropped near his body was
a portable radio receiver mechanically locked to an off-band frequency, and an uncatalogued teletype
message was intercepted: "You were looking at the wrong person."

A digital investigation terminal is opened for school student investigators. Operating under the
procedural oversight of The Director of Police, student operatives examine recovered crime scene

clippings across seven 48-hour investigation waves.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE UNDERLYING MYSTERY: WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The core narrative revelation of Case TLS-0017 is that the modern "Zodiac signals" were never
transmitted by an immortal historical killer or a distant fugitive. Instead, the entire phenomenon
is an INTERNAL CRIMINOLOGICAL CONSPIRACY operated from inside the Department of Archival
Investigation itself.

Beginning in October 1971, historical researcher Ellen Vale noticed that original Case 17 records
were subject to unauthorized chronological revisions: memos had margins aligned with secret keys,
and teletype lines had been tampered with. She documented her findings in a private journal (EV-0701)
and compiled an optical reel predicting that future investigators would follow the planted breadcrumb
trail rather than questioning the authority of the archives.

In October 2026, 34-year-old municipal archivist Daniel Mercer noticed that document entries
in the digital repository were being altered in real time: timestamps were being adjusted to predate
real-world police dispatches. Before Mercer could bring his findings to external authorities, he was
lured to an annex near Presidio Heights and killed.

The antagonist—an internal operative within the records department—planted the dropped radio, the
altered Exorcist memorandum, the negative telemetry coordinates, and the reversed cassette tape to
conduct a structured psychological test on incoming student investigators. The antagonist wanted to
prove that people would blindly chase historical ghosts rather than noticing that the evidence was
being fabricated by the very institution hosting the investigation.

When the student investigator solves Stage 6 and penetrates Ellen Vale's locked files, the antagonist
realizes the operative has reached the core secret. The Directorate Emergency Lockdown protocol is
abruptly initiated, ordering the investigator to cease inquiries and delete local files. Simultaneously,
a 22-year-old police recruit assigned to records duty—who assisted in transferring the files—is found
dead inside the lower archival vault, with an operating teletype receiver repeating the final carrier
signal.

The final conclusion players reach is embodied in the synthesis sentence:
"THE LAST SIGNAL CAME FROM INSIDE THE ARCHIVE."
